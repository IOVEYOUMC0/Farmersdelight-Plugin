# Mushroom Colonies

Mushroom colonies are dense clusters of mushrooms that grow on the pack's fertile soils and can be harvested again and again — a renewable mushroom farm without needing a huge dark room.

There are two: `farmersdelight:brown_mushroom_colony` and `farmersdelight:red_mushroom_colony`.

## Getting a colony

There are three ways to start one:

1. **Craft it.** Combine a mushroom block with a matching loose mushroom (shapeless):
   - `minecraft:brown_mushroom_block` + `minecraft:brown_mushroom` → `farmersdelight:brown_mushroom_colony`
   - `minecraft:red_mushroom_block` + `minecraft:red_mushroom` → `farmersdelight:red_mushroom_colony`
2. **Grow one from a mushroom.** Plant a plain brown or red mushroom on **rich soil** or **organic compost**. On a later random tick the fertile soil turns that mushroom into a **young (empty) colony**, which then grows on its own.
3. **Find one in the wild.** Brown and red mushroom colonies generate naturally in **mushroom fields** biomes.

## Where and how they grow

- Colonies grow on **`farmersdelight:rich_soil`** and **`farmersdelight:organic_compost`**.
- They grow in **any light level, including complete darkness** — no need to light or darken the area.
- A colony fills up over 4 stages (age 0–3). Each stage holds more mushrooms. Bone meal advances it toward full.

## Harvesting

A full-grown colony can be harvested two ways, and both leave the colony in place so it can regrow:

- **Shears (right-click):** trims **one** mushroom off, dropping a single mushroom and stepping the colony down by one stage. Good for a steady trickle.
- **A knife (right-click):** strips the colony bare in one go, dropping all of its mushrooms at once and resetting it to empty.

If you simply **break** the colony block:

- with **shears** while it is full, you recover the colony block itself (to move it),
- otherwise it shatters into loose mushrooms — more mushrooms the fuller it was (up to 5 from a full colony).

{% hint style="info" %}
Because colonies grow in the dark on rich soil or compost, a compact setup is: a floor of rich soil or organic compost, colonies planted on it, and a hopper or [Basket](storage.md#the-basket) beneath your harvest spot to gather the drops.
{% endhint %}
