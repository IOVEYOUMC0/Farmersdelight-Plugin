# Advancements

Farmer's Delight comes with its own advancement tree — a **Farmer's Delight** tab
alongside the vanilla ones — that guides you through the mod's content, from your first
knife to becoming a Master Chef. There are 23 advancements in total. You earn them just by
playing; each pops a toast and, for most, a chat message.

The root, **Farmer's Delight**, is granted the moment you join, and the tree branches into
three lines of progression: a **knife / harvesting** line, a **cooking** line, and a
**farming** line.

## The knife & harvesting line

Starts with **Hunt and Gather** (`craft_knife`) — craft any knife.

- **Wild Butcher** (`get_ham`) — obtain ham (a knife drop from pigs/hoglins, or its cooked
  and glazed forms).
- **Watch Your Fingers** (`use_cutting_board`) — use a cutting board.
  - **If You Can't Take the Heat...** (`obtain_netherite_knife`) — a *challenge*: obtain the
    netherite knife.
- **Grasping at Straws** (`harvest_straw`) — harvest straw with a knife.
  - **Advanced Composting** (`place_organic_compost`) — place organic compost.

## The cooking line

Starts with **Bonfire Lit** (`place_campfire`) — place a campfire.

- **Portable Cooking** (`use_skillet`) — cook something on a skillet.
  - **Sizzling Hot!** (`place_skillet`) — place a skillet.
- **Dinner's Served!** (`place_cooking_pot`) — a *goal*: place a cooking pot.
  - **Nourishing!** (`eat_nourishing_food`) — eat a meal that grants the Nourishment
    effect.
  - **A Glorious Feast** (`place_feast`) — place a feast block.
    - **Master Chef** (`master_chef`) — a *challenge*: eat every one of the mod's cooked
      dishes.

## The farming line

Starts with **Crops of the Wild** (`get_fd_seed`) — obtain one of the new seeds/crops
(cabbage seeds, tomato seeds, onion or rice).

- **Dipping Your Roots** (`plant_rice`) — plant rice.
  - **Crop Rotation** (`plant_all_crops`) — a *challenge*: plant every crop type.
- **Composting Begins** (`get_organic_compost`) — obtain organic compost.
  - **Black Gold** (`get_rich_soil`) — a *goal*: obtain rich soil.
    - **Premium Farmland** (`hoe_rich_soil`) — a *challenge*: hoe rich soil into farmland.
- **Fungus Among Us** (`get_mushroom_colony`) — obtain a mushroom colony.
- **Tall-mato** (`harvest_ropelogged_tomato`) — harvest a rope-grown tomato.
- **Boo! Hiss!** (`hit_raider_with_rotten_tomato`) — hit a raider with a thrown rotten
  tomato.

## Challenges and goals

Three advancements are the tree's showpieces. **Master Chef** requires you to eat *every*
Farmer's Delight dish, and **Crop Rotation** requires you to plant *every* crop — both track
their sub-tasks individually, so you can chip away at them over many sessions. **If You
Can't Take the Heat...** rewards the top-tier netherite knife.

## Server owners can turn them off

The advancement tree is optional. A server owner can disable the whole system, in which case
the Farmer's Delight tab and all its advancements simply do not appear — this does not affect
any actual gameplay, only the tracking. Owners can also keep or hide individual advancements.
If your server does not show this tab, it has been switched off in the configuration; the
food, tools and mechanics all still work regardless.
