# Player Guide

Everything Farmer's Delight adds to the game, explained for players: the cooking
stations, the tools, the crops and soil you farm, and the food and effects that keep you
fed on long adventures.

You do not need to read this in order — jump to whatever you are holding or standing in
front of. If you are brand new, start with **[The Cooking Pot](cooking-pot.md)**; it is
the heart of the mod, and most recipes flow through it.
