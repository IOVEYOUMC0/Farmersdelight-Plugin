# The Stove

The **Stove** is your dedicated heat source. It does two jobs:

1. It grills food placed on its top surface (like a campfire, but tidier and
   directional).
2. It acts as the **heat source** underneath a Cooking Pot or Skillet.

## Crafting a Stove

Shaped recipe (crafting table):

```
[ iron ] [ iron    ] [ iron ]
[ brick ] [        ] [ brick ]
[ brick ] [ campfire ] [ brick ]
```

- `I` = `minecraft:iron_ingot`
- `B` = `minecraft:bricks` (the block)
- `C` = `minecraft:campfire`

Result: `farmersdelight:stove` ×1.

## Lighting and extinguishing

A stove is placed **already lit**. You can toggle its fire by hand:

| Action | Item used | Result |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Light it | **Flint and Steel** | Lights the fire (costs 1 durability) |
| Light it | **Fire Charge** | Lights the fire (consumes the charge) |
| Put it out | **Shovel** (any) | Extinguishes it (costs 1 durability) |
| Put it out | **Water Bucket** | Extinguishes it and returns an empty bucket |

When lit, the stove glows, gives off light, and shows flames and smoke. Only a **lit**
stove cooks food and only a lit stove counts as a heat source.

## Grilling food on top

1. With the stove **lit**, right-click its top with a food item that has a campfire
   cooking recipe (raw meat, raw fish, potatoes, and so on).
2. The food sits on the grill and cooks over time. The stove has **six grilling
   slots**, so you can cook several items at once.
3. When an item finishes, the cooked result **pops out** on top of the stove for you
   to pick up.

If the fire goes out mid-cook, progress slowly cools back down until you relight it.
If a solid block is placed over the stove's grilling surface, the food is ejected.

Food placed on a stove can't be taken back by hand — it stays on the grill until it
finishes cooking and pops out on its own. Breaking the stove drops whatever is still
grilling on it.

## Using it as a heat source

Place a **Cooking Pot** or **Skillet** directly on top of a lit stove and it will cook.
This is the cleanest way to power a kitchen — see the Cooking Pot and Skillet pages.

## Careful — a lit stove burns you

Standing on the central grilling area of a **lit** stove deals fire damage (about half
a heart per hit, roughly 2 damage per second while you stand on it). This applies to
players and mobs alike.

- **Sneaking** onto a lit stove is safe — you won't be burned while crouched.
- Standing on the **rim** (the outer edge) is safe; only the central grill burns.
- Creative and Spectator players are never burned.

Put the stove out (shovel or water bucket) before walking across it, or sneak.

## Tips

- Keep a stove permanently lit under your cooking pots so meals never stall.
- The stove grills up to six items at once, making it better than a single campfire
  for batch-cooking meat and fish.
- Remember the burn: don't build a walkway across lit stoves, and warn other players —
  sneaking is the safe way across.
